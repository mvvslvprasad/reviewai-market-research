# Research Methodology

## Overview

This document outlines the research methodology applied in producing the **ReviewAI Tech & Solutions Market Research Report** as part of the CodeAlpha Business & Marketing Strategy Internship (Task 1).

---

## Research Design

**Type:** Descriptive + Exploratory Mixed Research  
**Scope:** Freelancing Industry & AI SaaS Startup Ecosystem (Global)  
**Period:** 2021–2027 (historical data + projections)  
**Focus Company:** ReviewAI Tech & Solutions

---

## Phase 1 — Industry Scoping

- Defined the industry boundaries: intersection of **freelancing economy** and **AI-powered review analytics**
- Identified primary SIC codes: 7372 (Prepackaged Software), 7374 (Computer Processing and Data Preparation)
- Defined Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

---

## Phase 2 — SWOT Analysis Framework

Applied the classic **Andrews SWOT Framework** with internal/external distinction:

| Dimension | Source Type | Inputs |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Strengths | Internal | Product capabilities, technology stack, operational model |
| Weaknesses | Internal | Stage limitations, resource constraints, market position |
| Opportunities | External | Market projections, trend data, whitespace analysis |
| Threats | External | Competitive intelligence, regulatory landscape, macroeconomic factors |

---

## Phase 3 — Target Audience Segmentation

Applied **Demographic + Psychographic + Behavioral Segmentation**:

1. Identified 5 primary customer segments
2. Modeled Willingness to Pay (WTP) ranges based on comparable SaaS pricing benchmarks
3. Assigned priority scores based on: (a) addressable volume, (b) acquisition cost, (c) LTV potential

---

## Phase 4 — Competitive Landscape Analysis

Framework: **Multi-Dimensional Benchmarking**

Dimensions evaluated:
1. AI Accuracy (NLP model performance)
2. Pricing accessibility
3. UX/Design quality
4. Platform integrations
5. Processing speed
6. Customer support quality

Scored each competitor 0–100 per dimension based on publicly available data, user reviews (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot), and product documentation.

---

## Phase 5 — Market Trend Analysis

**Trend Momentum Index** calculated as a composite score:

```
Momentum Score = (Adoption Rate × 0.4) + (Search Volume Growth × 0.3) + (Investment Flow × 0.3)
```

Data sourced from:
- Statista (2024–2025 editions)
- Grand View Research AI Analytics Report 2025
- McKinsey Global Institute: State of AI 2024
- World Economic Forum Future of Jobs 2025
- Upwork Freelance Forward Annual Report 2024

---

## Data Validation

- Cross-referenced all market size figures across minimum 2 independent sources
- Competitive scores verified against G2 and Capterra user reviews (n > 500 per product)
- All projections use conservative estimates (lower bound of research range)

---

## Limitations

1. ReviewAI is a hypothetical/early-stage company; some internal data is modeled, not reported
2. Market projections are subject to macroeconomic variance
3. Competitive scores reflect public data only; proprietary internal metrics not available
4. Regional variance in freelancing adoption rates is generalized at global level

---

## Citation Standards

All external statistics cited with source, year, and access context. Figures represent original research synthesis, not direct reproduction.
